At a glance

Cartajlife runs a network of ambassadors who sell products and bring in new members. As they reach their sales objectives, ambassadors move up a ladder of grades (Standard, Manager, Leader), and each grade comes with its own default discount. Customers can be individuals or companies.

Cartajlife Admin is the web back office where the staff manage this network day to day: they create and update accounts, promote ambassadors, decide what each person can see or do, talk with members and review everything that happened in the system.

Who it's for

Network administrators

Manage every account, set up grades and promotions, and decide what staff and ambassadors are allowed to see or change.

Support and operations staff

Look up members, answer them through the built-in chat and keep personal and bank details up to date, within the rights they have been given.

Managers and auditors

Follow who did what and when through a filterable activity log covering sign-ins and every change.

The problem it solves

Many kinds of accounts

Ambassadors, individual clients, company clients and staff each need different information. The back office keeps them in one place, with forms that adapt to each type.

Who can see what

In a sales network, visibility matters: an ambassador should only see the team information they are entitled to, and each staff member only the actions their role allows.

Traceability

Grade changes, account updates and deletions need to be explainable later. Every sign-in and every change is recorded automatically.

Main features

Account management

Ambassadors, individual clients, company clients and staff in separate tabs, with search by name, email, ID card number or phone, status filters and pagination.

Adaptive forms

Bank details for ambassadors, company name and tax ID for companies, and a country, governorate and city selection that narrows down as you choose.

Grades and promotions

Grades with a default discount and a sales objective, manual promotion of an ambassador, and a full promotion history that can be filtered by ambassador or by mode.

Ambassador visibility rules

Per-ambassador switches for what their team data shows (invoices, orders, members, bonuses, challenges), filters by member grade, and exclusion of specific members.

Staff permissions

For each module, a staff member can be allowed to create, read, update or delete. Actions outside these rights are refused with a clear message.

Member chat

Conversations between the back office and members, with the last message in the list, a search to start a new chat and filtering of conversations.

Location reference data

Countries, governorates and cities managed in three linked levels, preloaded with the 24 Tunisian governorates and their cities.

Bank reference data

The list of banks used for ambassador bank details, with duplicates refused and banks in use protected from deletion.

Activity log

Every sign-in and every creation, update or deletion is recorded and can be filtered by module, action, user type and date range.

Works on phones

The whole back office adapts to small screens, with a slide-out navigation menu.

How it works

A staff member signs in

Only staff accounts can open the back office; ambassadors and clients are refused, and the session renews itself quietly while the person works.

Their rights are applied

Each request is checked against the person's permissions for that module, so they can only create, read, update or delete what they were granted.

They manage the network

Accounts, grades, visibility rules, locations and banks are edited through forms that check the data before saving it.

They talk with members

The chat keeps each conversation with its history, so any follow-up starts from the full context.

Everything is recorded

Each change is written to the activity log, which can be reviewed later with filters.

Technologies

Interface

Next.js and ReactBuild the back-office screens, navigation and side panels.
TypeScriptUsed on both the interface and the server to keep data shapes consistent.
Tailwind CSSProvides the visual style and the layout that adapts to phones.

Server & APIs

Node.js and ExpressServe the REST API that the back office calls for every action.
ZodChecks every form and request before the data is saved.

Data

MySQLStores accounts, permissions, grades, promotions, locations, banks and the activity log.
MongoDBStores chat conversations and messages.
Docker ComposeStarts both databases together for local work.

Security

JWT sessionsShort-lived sign-in tokens, renewed automatically through a protected cookie.
bcryptStores passwords in hashed form only.
Role-based access controlLimits the back office to staff and applies per-module permissions on every request.
