At a glance

Nozha Hotels brings together a group of two seaside hotels in Hammamet, Nozha Beach and Royal Nozha. A welcome page presents the group and lets visitors choose their hotel; each hotel then has its own site with rooms, photos, spa, restaurants and activities.

From any hotel site, travellers can check availability, build their stay, pay online on the bank's secure page and receive their confirmation by e-mail. Behind the scenes, each hotel runs its own back office for content, pricing, reservations and payment settings.

Who it's for

Travellers

Families and couples who want to compare rooms, see the exact price of their stay and book without calling the hotel.

Hotel teams

Reception, reservation and management staff who follow each booking and its payment, and set prices by season, room and meal plan.

Group marketing

The team that keeps the group welcome page up to date: hotel presentations, gallery, guest reviews, awards and newsletter.

The problem it solves

Bookings scattered across channels

Without its own booking engine, a hotel depends on phone calls, e-mails and intermediaries. The platform lets guests book and pay directly on the hotel's site.

Prices that change all the time

Rates depend on the season, the room, the meal plan and the number of guests. A central pricing grid keeps every quote consistent with what the hotel decided.

Several hotels, one group

Each hotel keeps its own data, prices and payment account, while the group presents all its hotels in one place.

Main features

Group welcome page

A shared entry point presenting the hotels, the group's story, a photo gallery, guest reviews, awards and a newsletter sign-up.

A site for each hotel

Rooms filtered by category with photos, size, capacity and starting price, plus pages for the spa, restaurants, activities and contact.

Guided booking

Dates, number of adults and children, choice of room and meal plan, with several rooms possible in the same booking.

Extras and events

Optional services such as an airport transfer and dated events such as a gala dinner, added to the stay with a total that updates immediately.

Online payment

The guest pays on the bank's secure page; the result is checked with the bank before the reservation is marked as paid.

Reservation page and documents

A personal reservation page, confirmation e-mails and PDF vouchers with a QR code, the stay summary and the cancellation policy.

Reservation management

A list of bookings filtered by status, with payment status, booking details, status changes and the option to resend a payment link or a voucher.

Pricing and availability

Daily prices by season, room and meal plan, room stock, minimum stays, stop sales, promotions and cancellation penalties.

Per-hotel payment settings

Each hotel has its own payment account and currency rates, so payments go to the right hotel.

Content in two languages

Hotel and group content is entered in English and French and visitors switch language at any time.

How it works

Choose a hotel

The visitor arrives on the group welcome page, picks a hotel and browses its rooms and services.

Search

They enter their dates and party; the platform shows the rooms available for those dates with the price for each meal plan.

Build the stay

They choose the room and meal plan, add extras or events, and see the total update as they go.

Pay securely

After entering their details, they are sent to the bank's secure payment page for the exact amount of the stay.

Receive the confirmation

The reservation page shows the booking and its status, and the guest receives an e-mail with a PDF voucher.

Follow up in the back office

Hotel staff see the new booking with its payment status, confirm it and send the final voucher.

Technologies

Interface

ReactBuilds the group page, the hotel sites, the booking flow and the back offices.
TypeScriptAdds types to the code on both the interface and the server side.
ViteRuns the interface during development and produces the version that goes online.

Server & APIs

Node.js and ExpressServe the data used by the sites and back offices, compute availability and prices, and handle bookings.
JSON Web TokensKeep each hotel's back office signed in and separate from the others.
Scheduled tasksRegularly re-check payments that are not yet finalised so that each booking shows its real status.

Data

MySQLStores content, prices, availability, reservations and payments, with separate data for each hotel.

Payments & documents

ClicToPayThe bank's payment gateway, where guests pay for their stay on a secure page.
PDFKit and QR codesGenerate the PDF vouchers sent to guests, each with a QR code linking to the reservation.
NodemailerSends booking confirmations, vouchers, payment reminders and the group newsletter.

What it brings

Direct bookings

Guests book and pay on the hotel's own site, at any time, and see the full total before paying.

Less manual follow-up

Payment status, confirmation e-mails and vouchers are handled by the platform, so staff focus on the guests.

Room to grow

Each hotel runs independently on the same platform, which makes it straightforward to add another hotel to the group.
