At a glance

ProHunting supports guided wild boar hunting stays in northern Tunisia. A stay combines accommodation, certified guides, transfers, hunting zones and official permits from the forestry authorities.

The platform covers the whole journey of a booking, from the hunter's first request to the end of the stay: quote, payments, permits, day-by-day planning, documents and the client's review. Each reservation moves through clear statuses (new, quote sent, confirmed, in progress, closed, cancelled) and keeps a timeline of every action.

Who it's for

Hunters and visitors

Browse stays, hunting zones on a map, partner hotels, guides and the season calendar, then send a stay request in their own language.

Clients

Follow their reservations, accept quotes, upload licences and passports, receive their stay planning and talk with the community in a private forum.

The operations team

Handles requests, quotes, payments, permits, logistics and documents from one back office, with access rights set per person.

Partner agencies

Bring in clients and are followed with their own commissions and statistics.

The problem it solves

Scattered follow-up

A hunting stay involves the client, hotels, guides, drivers and the forestry offices. Without a shared tool, each booking lives in emails, spreadsheets and paper forms.

Heavy paperwork

Every hunter needs an official permit request with passport and weapon details, and each day of hunting needs a regional authorisation. Filling these by hand for a group takes time and invites errors.

Little visibility

Without a single view, it is hard to know what has been paid, which zones are approved for which day, or how the season is going.

Main features

Public site

Stay catalogue with detailed pages, interactive map of hunting zones, partner hotels, guides, events calendar, gallery, blog and client reviews.

Guided stay request

Five steps: stay, dates from a real availability calendar, lead guest, group members and documents, with checks on group size and required details.

Client portal

Reservations with their history, shared documents, quotes to accept online, stay planning, real-time notifications, cancellation request and post-stay review.

Reservation follow-up

One page per booking gathers twelve actions: details, changes, documents, quotes, planning, client message, confirmation, complaint, payments, cancellation and more.

Quotes, payments and invoices

Versioned quotes, payments by method with a live remaining balance, and a single invoice updated after each payment.

Official documents

Permit requests per hunter or for the whole group, regional authorisation letters, booking certificates, road sheets and hotel lists, produced from the reservation data.

Day planning

For each day of the stay: authorisation requests, approved zones, vehicles and drivers, notes, accommodation and flights, then a planning sent to the client.

Reference data

Hunting zones drawn directly on a map, forests, guides, vehicles, hotels with their location, and document templates with merge fields.

Dashboard and exports

Activity indicators over a chosen period, charts, a stay calendar by status and exports to PDF and Excel.

Site content and community

Pages, blog, events and search settings edited by the team; review moderation, contact messages and a moderated private forum.

How it works

The hunter sends a request

On the public site, they pick a stay and dates, then enter the group and the details needed for the permits.

The team sends a quote

The quote goes to the client portal, where the client can accept it online. The reservation is then confirmed.

Payments are recorded

Each payment updates the remaining balance and the single invoice for the booking.

Permits and logistics are prepared

The team generates the official requests, sends them to the forestry offices, records the approved zones day by day and assigns vehicles, drivers and accommodation.

The stay takes place

The client receives the day-by-day planning and the reservation switches to in progress on the arrival date.

Closing and review

After the stay, the reservation is closed and the client can leave a review.

Technologies

Interface

Next.js and ReactPower the public site, the client portal and the back office in a single web application.
TypeScriptUsed on both the interface and the server for consistent, checked data.
Tailwind CSSGives every screen the same visual style.
next-intlServes the public site in French, English and German.
RechartsDraws the charts on the dashboard.

Server & APIs

Node.js and ExpressRun the API that holds the business rules: reservations, quotes, payments, planning and documents.
ZodChecks every piece of data received before it is processed.
JWT, two-factor and Google sign-inHandle secure sign-in for clients and staff, with rights set per permission.
Scheduled tasksMove confirmed reservations to in progress on the arrival day.

Data

MariaDB / MySQLStore reservations, clients, zones, documents and site content.
SequelizeConnects the server to the database and keeps deleted records recoverable.

Maps & documents

MapboxDisplays hunting zones and hotels, and lets the team draw zone outlines on the map.
PuppeteerProduces branded PDF quotes, invoices, certificates, road sheets and official forms.
ExcelJS and ZIP archivesExport data to spreadsheets and bundle a booking's documents in one download.
NodemailerSends transactional emails such as quotes, plannings and authorisation requests.

Real time

Socket.IOUpdates badges, notifications, reservation timelines and the forum without reloading the page.

Deployment

nginx and pm2Serve the site and the API over HTTPS on a Linux server and keep them running.

What it brings

One tool from request to closing

Requests, quotes, payments, permits, planning and documents are in one place, with no re-typing between files.

Official documents in one click

Ministry forms, regional letters, road sheets and invoices are filled from the booking, for one hunter or a whole group.

Administrative follow-up

Permits are tracked by zone and by day, and required details such as passports and weapons are checked upfront.

A clear client experience

A guided request, then a personal space with documents, quotes, planning and live notifications.

Team autonomy

Site content, document templates, zones, hotels, guides and user rights are managed without a developer.

Traceability

Each reservation keeps a timeline of who did what and when, and sensitive operations are logged.
