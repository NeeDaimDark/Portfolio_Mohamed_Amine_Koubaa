At a glance

PhotoEvent is a web platform for event photography. Photographers publish the galleries of weddings, seminars, galas or festivals; guests find the photos in which they appear, buy them as digital files or prints, and can then rate the photographer.

The platform covers the whole cycle after an event: protected publication, search by face, cart and payment, delivery of the original files, receipts and invoices. Photographers also get a professional space with a public showcase page, a booking calendar, revenue tracking and statistics, and an administration team validates photographers, moderates reviews and manages pricing and payouts.

The application is responsive, works on desktop and mobile, and is available in French and English with a light and a dark theme.

Who it's for

Photographers

Independent wedding and event photographers or studios who want to sell their photos, receive booking requests and follow their revenue in one place.

Guests and clients

Couples, guests and participants who want to find the photos taken of them at an event and order them as files or prints.

Event organisers

Companies and organisers of seminars, galas or festivals who need a simple way to share event photos with participants.

Platform administrators

The team that validates photographer applications, moderates reviews and complaints, and manages plans, pricing, payouts and invoices.

The problem it solves

Photos scattered everywhere

After an event, hundreds of photos usually circulate by USB stick, messaging apps or shared drives, with no control and no payment.

Hard to find your own photos

Guests have to scroll through entire albums to find the few pictures in which they appear.

Lost sales

Screenshots and copies let people keep photos without paying the photographer.

Too many separate tools

Bookings, messages, payments and invoices are usually spread across several tools that do not talk to each other.

Main features

Protected galleries

Photos are shown as watermarked previews, the full-screen viewer blurs the image until the visitor presses and holds, and capture attempts are blocked and counted. Originals stay private until purchase.

Face recognition

A guest turns on the camera and the platform gathers the photos of the event in which they appear, ready to be added to the cart.

Event sharing

Each event can be public or reserved to a private link, and is shared with a link, a share code or a downloadable QR code.

Cart and checkout

Guests choose a digital or printed copy, then pay online or in cash; cash orders are confirmed by the photographer. Paid photos, contract and receipt are then available for download.

Showcase and bookings

Every photographer has a public page with specialities, galleries and reviews, where visitors can request a date. A calendar shows open, closed, booked and pending slots.

Gallery customisation

Photographers upload files or whole folders and adjust the look of each gallery with ready-made themes and colours, previewed on desktop and mobile.

Revenue, payouts and invoices

Photographers see their available and pending balance, request payouts and download their invoices and receipts as PDF files.

Statistics

Monthly revenue, photos sold, gallery views, blocked capture attempts and downloads, with a breakdown by event type and a CSV export.

Verified reviews

Only clients with a paid order or a confirmed booking can rate a photographer, and every review is checked automatically by AI before it is published.

Messaging, notifications and complaints

Built-in conversations between clients and photographers, in-app and email notifications, and a complaint system with follow-up by the administration.

How it works

The photographer joins

A photographer signs up and submits an application with legal documents; an administrator reviews it and validates the account.

An event is published

In a three-step wizard, the photographer describes the event, sets the prices for digital and printed copies, chooses the access mode and uploads the photos.

The platform prepares the gallery

Originals are stored privately, watermarked and blurred previews are generated, and faces are indexed so that guests can search by face.

Guests find and order

Guests open the link, code or QR code, browse the protected gallery or use face recognition, select their photos and choose digital or print.

Payment and delivery

The client pays online or in cash. Once the order is paid, the originals can be downloaded with a receipt, and the sale appears in the photographer's revenue.

Feedback and follow-up

The client can leave a review, checked automatically before publication, while the photographer follows statistics, payouts and monthly invoices.

Server & APIs

PythonLanguage of the business services: events, files, orders, payments, plans, reviews and more.
AWS Lambda with ChaliceRuns each business area as an independent serverless service.
Amazon API GatewaySingle REST entry point for the web application.
FastAPISmall service that returns the moderation decision for each review.

Data & storage

MongoDBStores users, events, orders, bookings, reviews, finances and conversations.
Amazon S3Keeps original photos in private storage, separate from watermarked previews, posters and invoices.
Amazon SQSQueues slow tasks such as watermarking, emails, notifications and review moderation.

AI & image

Amazon RekognitionIndexes the faces of each event and finds the photos matching a guest's face.
Large language modelsClassify reviews to reject insults, hate speech and spam before publication.

Identity & payments

Amazon CognitoSign-up, sign-in, password recovery and role-based access.
PaymeeOnline payment of photo orders and photographer subscriptions.

What it brings

For guests

A link or a code is enough to reach the gallery, face recognition finds the right photos, and payment works online or in cash.

For photographers

One professional space for showcase, bookings, protected galleries, sales, revenue and invoices, without any technical tool to manage.

Protected work

Watermarks, blur and capture blocking reduce unpaid copies, and originals are delivered only after payment.

Trust

Reviews come only from real clients and are moderated before publication, which makes ratings more reliable.

For the platform team

Photographer validation, review moderation, versioned pricing, payouts and complaints are handled from a single administration space.
