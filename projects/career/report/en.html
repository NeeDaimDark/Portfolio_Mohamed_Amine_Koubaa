At a glance

Career is a web platform that covers recruitment from the published offer to the final decision. Candidates browse offers, apply with their CV, follow each application step by step and prepare with an AI assistant, AI-assisted interviews and learning paths toward the job they want.

Recruiters and HR teams publish offers in a few clicks, receive applicants' CVs already scored by AI, compare two profiles side by side and record their decision. An admin mode adds profile verification and a view of AI model usage.

Who it's for

Candidates

Job seekers who search offers, apply, track their applications, manage their CVs and train toward a target position.

Recruiters and HR

Teams that publish offers, review scored CVs, compare applicants, browse the CV library and follow employees' skills and learning paths.

Administrators

People who verify candidate documents such as visas, residence cards and work permits, and follow how AI models are used on the platform.

The problem it solves

Too many CVs to read

Recruiters receive more applications than they can review, and comparing candidates by hand takes time.

Little feedback for candidates

Applicants rarely know where their application stands or how well their profile fits an offer.

Training not tied to a goal

Learning is seldom linked to the position a person is aiming for, and progress is hard to measure.

Main features

Job offers

Search by skill, technology or company, with salary, contract type, location and required skills on each offer.

Application tracking

Each application shows a status timeline: sent, CV viewed, interview, final decision, with totals by status.

CV management

Upload CVs in PDF or Word format, choose whether each one is public or private, and chat with the AI about a given CV.

AI assistant per offer

Opened from an offer, the assistant explains how well the candidate fits, based on their own applications and scores.

AI-assisted interviews

A written interview with technical, behavioural, language and writing questions, or a phone interview led by the AI.

Learning paths

From the current to the target position: a roadmap of chapters, a PDF guide per chapter and AI-generated quizzes.

Publish an offer

Recruiters add an offer from a link, a pasted description, a screenshot or a full form; the AI fills in the details.

Scoring and comparison

Applicants' CVs receive an AI score; two candidates can be compared on scores, strengths and weaknesses before the decision.

CV library and skills matrix

A searchable library of public CVs, plus a skills matrix per employee with CSV export.

Admin mode

Profile verification (visa, residence, work permit) and AI model usage: tokens, calls, estimated cost, errors and latency per model.

How it works

A recruiter publishes an offer

From a link, text, image or form. The AI extracts the title, skills and conditions, and the offer goes online.

A candidate applies

They can ask the assistant whether the offer suits them, then apply with a CV. The CV is analysed in the background.

An AI-assisted interview takes place

In writing in the browser, or by phone after entering a personal code. The answers feed an evaluation report.

The recruiter compares and decides

Applicants appear with their AI score. Two profiles can be compared side by side, and the recruiter accepts or rejects.

The candidate follows up and progresses

The status timeline updates at each step, and a learning path can help them grow toward the position they want.

What it brings

Faster screening

Recruiters start from scored CVs and a structured comparison instead of reading every application.

Explained decisions

Scores come with strengths and weaknesses, and the final decision stays with the recruiter.

Transparency for candidates

Each application shows where it stands, and the assistant explains how well a profile fits an offer.

Growth toward a target job

Learning paths turn a career goal into chapters, guides and quizzes with visible progress.
